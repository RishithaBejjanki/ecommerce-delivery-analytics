# Data quality log

Every cleaning decision, how many records it touched, and why.
Counts are generated by [`sql/03_data_quality.sql`](../sql/03_data_quality.sql) and saved to
[`outputs/data_quality_checks.csv`](../outputs/data_quality_checks.csv), so this log can't drift from the code.

Rule of thumb used throughout: **flag, don't silently delete.** Raw rows stay in the staging
tables with flags, and the `in_analysis` column on `fact_orders` decides what the analysis uses.

## Definitions that affect the numbers
- **Late** = delivered on a calendar date after the promised (estimated) date. Same-day counts as on time.
- **Seller missed SLA** = handed to carrier after the order's earliest item `shipping_limit_date`.
- **Stage durations** use minute-level timestamps converted to fractional days.
